## 💡 What This Project Does

This dashboard analyzes **employee attrition patterns** and converts them into:

* 📊 Clear business insights
* 🎯 Retention-focused KPIs
* 💼 Actionable HR strategies

👉 Built to support **data-driven decision-making**, not just visualization.

---

## 🚨 Business Problem

Employee churn leads to:

* Increased hiring costs
* Productivity loss
* Knowledge drain

However, organizations often lack:

❌ Clear visibility into churn drivers
❌ Data-backed retention strategies
❌ Real-time performance insights

---

## 🎯 Solution

An interactive **Power BI dashboard** that:

✅ Identifies key churn drivers
✅ Highlights high-risk employee segments
✅ Enables HR teams to take proactive action
✅ Provides intuitive, real-time insights

---

## ⚡ Key Features

### 📈 Income vs Attrition Analysis

* Compare average income across employees
* Identify whether compensation impacts retention

---

### 😊 Job Satisfaction Insights

* Analyze satisfaction levels by job role
* Detect roles with high dissatisfaction risk

---

### 🕒 Work-Life Balance Impact

* Correlate work-life balance with tenure
* Identify burnout-driven attrition patterns

---

### 🏢 Department & Role Breakdown

* Segment employees by department
* Identify high-risk business units

---

### 🎛 Interactive Dashboard

* Dynamic filters
* Drill-down capabilities
* KPI-driven visuals

👉 Designed for **quick executive decision-making**

---

## 📊 Key Business Insights

* Low job satisfaction strongly correlates with higher attrition
* Poor work-life balance reduces employee retention
* Income alone is not a reliable predictor of churn
* Certain roles and departments carry higher risk

👉 Insight: **Employee experience matters more than compensation**
